# 📘 User Manual & Operating Guide

---

## 1. Getting Started
1. Open your browser and navigate to `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Click **Sign In** or **Register** to create an account.

---

## 2. Operating the Live Camera Detector
1. Click **Live Camera** in the navigation bar or dashboard.
2. Grant camera permissions when prompted.
3. The AI engine will stream live video, draw bounding box overlays around detected potholes, and trigger an audio warning beep if a Critical hazard is identified.

---

## 3. Uploading Road Images & Videos
1. Click **Upload Image** or **Upload Video**.
2. Drag and drop your image file (`.jpg`, `.png`) or dashcam video (`.mp4`).
3. Click **Run Pothole Detector**.
4. View side-by-side original vs annotated bounding box results, confidence scores, and estimated surface area in $m^2$.

---

## 4. Interactive GIS Map
1. Navigate to **GIS Map**.
2. Filter hazards by severity level (*Critical*, *High*, *Medium*, *Low*).
3. Click any marker to view location details, image snapshot, and current repair status.
