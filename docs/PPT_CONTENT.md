## Slide 2: Problem Statement
- Potholes cause over $3 Billion in annual vehicle damages.
- Manual inspection is slow, hazardous, and reactive.
- Lack of real-time hazard warnings for approaching drivers.

---

## Slide 3: Proposed Solution
- Automated AI detection using **YOLOv8** & **OpenCV**.
- Live webcam streaming & dashcam video processing.
- Interactive GIS hazard heatmap & location tagging.
- Real-time audio alerts & push notifications.
